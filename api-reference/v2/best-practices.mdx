LinkupAPI connects to third-party platforms like LinkedIn, WhatsApp and iMessage. Each provider enforces its own limits and restrictions. Understanding these limits is essential to avoid account warnings or suspensions.

<Warning>
  The limits below are **conservative recommendations**. You may increase them at your own risk. Exceeding provider limits will result in HTTP `429` or `500` errors. Make sure your system handles these errors gracefully.
</Warning>

## General Principles

These principles apply to **all providers and all routes**:

- **Space out your calls** — Use random delays between requests instead of fixed intervals. Predictable patterns are easily flagged as automation.
- **Distribute across working hours** — Spread your actions throughout the day during business hours rather than sending everything at once.
- **Warm up new accounts** — For new or inactive accounts, start with low volumes and increase gradually over 2-3 weeks. Accounts with fewer than ~150 connections may experience delivery issues with invitations.
- **Use real accounts only** — Fake accounts are well-detected by platforms, even without automation. We strongly recommend using only genuine accounts.
- **All limits are per account** — They are independent of the API route and do not accumulate across endpoints.

## LinkedIn Limits

### Connection Requests (Invitations)

| Account Type | Daily Limit | Weekly Limit | Note Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid LinkedIn** (Premium, Business, etc.) | 80–100 invitations | ~200/week | Up to 300 characters |
| **Free LinkedIn** | ~5 invitations/month (with note) | ~150/week (without note) | Up to 200 characters |

<Info>
  When you hit LinkedIn's invitation limit, you'll receive a `429 / RATE_LIMITED` error — back off and retry later. We don't enforce any limits on our side — you'll hit the exact same limit as in the LinkedIn UI. You can verify by trying the same action directly in the LinkedIn interface.

  A `422 / CHANNEL_ERROR` mentioning `CANT_RESEND_YET` is not a quota: after withdrawing an invitation, LinkedIn only lets you invite the same member again about 3 weeks later.
</Info>

**Recommendations:**
- Withdraw stale invitations older than 2-3 weeks using the [Withdraw](/api-reference/v2/network/withdraw) endpoint
- Target relevant profiles — random invitations increase rejection rates and trigger LinkedIn restrictions

### Profile Retrieval

| Action | Recommended Daily Limit |
|---|---|
| Profile views / profile data | ~100 per account per day |

This is a conservative recommendation. We don't enforce this limit, but exceeding it may trigger LinkedIn restrictions on your account.

### Search Results

There are **two separate limits** to consider:

**Per-search cap (LinkedIn limit):**
- Standard LinkedIn search: max **1,000 profiles** returned per search query
- Even if the UI shows more total results, LinkedIn only returns the first 1,000

<Tip>
  To retrieve more results than the per-search cap, narrow your query using filters (location, industry, company, etc.) and perform multiple searches.
</Tip>

**Daily usage (recommendation):**
- We recommend limiting the total number of profiles retrieved to **~1,000 per day** per account
- This refers to the total result lines actually fetched, not the number of API requests

### Connections & Invitation Lists

If you need to detect accepted invitations, **avoid polling at fixed intervals** (e.g., every hour or at 8:00 AM daily) — this pattern is easily flagged as automation.

**Recommended approach:**
1. Use the [Webhook](/api-reference/v2/webhooks/introduction) `accepted_invitation` event to detect accepted invitations

### Messaging

**Recommendations:**
- Space actions randomly throughout the day
- Limit to **30–50 messages per day** per account for optimal deliverability
- Personalize every message — generic copy gets flagged and ignored
- Wait 3-7 days before following up, and limit follow-ups to 2-3 per contact

### InMail

The number of InMails you can send depends on your LinkedIn subscription. Even without remaining credits, you can still send **free InMails to open profiles**.

**Recommendations:**
- Space sends randomly throughout the day
- Limit daily sends to **30–50** to maximize your monthly allocation

### Content & Posts

- **1-2 posts per day** maximum — more than that reduces engagement
- Vary your content format (text, articles, media)
- For comments and reactions: follow the general limit of ~100 per day per account

### Other LinkedIn Routes

For any route not listed above, follow the same principles:
- Minimize usage as much as possible
- Space out calls at random intervals
- Default limit: **~100 actions per day per account** (e.g., retrieving company profiles, fetching posts, adding reactions, commenting)

## WhatsApp Limits

WhatsApp enforces strict anti-spam measures. Follow these guidelines:

| Action | Recommended Limit |
|---|---|
| New conversations | 20–30 per day |
| Messages to existing chats | 100 per day |

- **Don't message contacts who haven't opted in** — WhatsApp bans accounts aggressively for unsolicited messages
- **Use random delays** between messages (10-30 seconds minimum)
- **Warm up new numbers** — Start with 5-10 messages/day and increase over several weeks

## iMessage Limits

An iMessage line is a dedicated phone number. Messaging is unlimited; what is limited is how many **different contacts** a line reaches, and how fast it **opens new conversations**. LinkupAPI checks the sending rules before each send and refuses a send that would break them, with the reason in `error.sub_code` (see below).

### Contacts per line

| Limit | Per line |
|---|---|
| Different contacts | 300 per rolling 24 hours, 1,000 per month |

- Every contact the line sends to counts once in the period, including contacts who wrote first. Messaging the same contact again does not count twice.
- Once a limit is reached, the line still **receives** messages but can no longer **reply** until the period frees up.

### Opening conversations

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| New conversations | up to **50 per day** per line |
| Interval between two new conversations | 15 minutes |
| Follow-ups to a contact who has not replied | 2 at most, 24 hours apart |

- **Replies don't count.** Once a contact has answered, messages to them no longer use the new-conversation quota. A reply within 24 hours of their last message goes out with no interval; after 24 hours of silence, such messages are spaced 2 minutes apart.
- **First message in plain text.** Until the contact replies, a message cannot contain a link, an email address, a phone number, an amount of money, or an attachment. Write something the contact would answer, and let them say if they don't want to hear from you.
- **Reply rate.** Keep it above roughly 30%. Message contacts who recently opted in or gave you their consent.
- A follow-up to a contact who never replied counts as a new conversation.

### Ready numbers and new numbers

| | Ready number | New number |
|---|---|---|
| Age | Usually 1+ months | Brand new |
| New conversations per day | 50 from day one | Days 1–2: 2 · Days 3–4: 5 · Days 5–7: 10 · Days 8–14: 20 · Days 15–21: 30 · Then: 50 |
| Number | Picked from the available numbers, no area code choice | Assigned by the carrier |
| Activation | Within a minute | Within a few hours |

A ready number may have had another owner: from time to time, it can receive messages from contacts of that owner. A new number is assigned when no ready number is available in the country.

`get_me` on an iMessage account returns `sending_limits`: the day of the line, new conversations allowed, used and left in the last 24 hours, the reply rate, and whether the line is warmed up.

### Refused sends

| `error.sub_code` | HTTP | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `IMESSAGE_DAILY_NEW_CONTACT_LIMIT` | 429 | New conversations of the last 24 hours used up |
| `IMESSAGE_NEW_CONTACT_INTERVAL` | 429 | Less than 15 minutes since the last new conversation |
| `IMESSAGE_FOLLOW_UP_TOO_SOON` | 429 | Less than 24 hours since the last follow-up to this contact |
| `IMESSAGE_FOLLOW_UP_INTERVAL` | 429 | Less than 2 minutes since the last message to a contact quiet for 24 hours |
| `IMESSAGE_RECIPIENT_MUST_REPLY` | 422 | 3 messages without a reply: wait for theirs |
| `IMESSAGE_FIRST_MESSAGE_CONTENT` | 400 | Link, email, phone number, amount or attachment before the contact replied |
| `IMESSAGE_SEND_REFUSED` | 422 | Refused by the carrier |

The `429` messages say how many seconds to wait before retrying.

## Architecture Recommendations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use a Job Queue" icon="list-check">
    Process API calls through a queue (Redis, RabbitMQ, etc.) with random delays between jobs. This prevents bursts and makes retry logic simpler.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cache Aggressively" icon="database">
    Profile and company data don't change every minute. Cache results locally to save credits and reduce unnecessary API calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use Webhooks" icon="bell">
    Instead of polling for new messages or accepted invitations, set up [Webhooks](/api-reference/v2/webhooks/introduction) for real-time notifications. It's safer and more efficient.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
