---
title: 'Get Group Members'
api: 'POST /v2/network'
description: 'List the members of a LinkedIn group from its URL.'
---

## Overview

Returns the members of a LinkedIn group, paginated with `count` and `offset`. **The account must be a member of the group**: LinkedIn only shows a group's member list to its members.

<Note>This endpoint consumes **1 credit per 10 results** returned. Ignore this if you're on per-seat pricing: usage is unlimited.</Note>

## Request

<ParamField header="x-api-key" type="string" required>
  Your API key for authentication.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the account to use.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="action" type="string" required>
  Must be `"get_group_members"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="params" type="object" required>
  <Expandable title="params properties">
    <ParamField body="group_url" type="string" required>
      The group URL (`https://www.linkedin.com/groups/8561802/`) or its numeric id. `group_id` is accepted as an alias.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="count" type="integer" default={10}>
      Number of members to return. Capped at `1000` per call; paginate with `offset`.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="offset" type="integer" default={0}>
      Number of members to skip.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="first_degree_only" type="boolean" default={false}>
      Only return members who are 1st-degree connections of the account.
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="data properties">
    <ResponseField name="profiles" type="array">
      The members: `name`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `job_title`, `connection_level`, `profile_url`, `public_identifier`, `profile_urn`, `profile_id`, `profile_picture`, `group_role` (`MEMBER`, `MANAGER`, `OWNER`) and `joined_at`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_results" type="integer">Number of members returned.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_available_results" type="integer">Total number of members in the group.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

If the account is not a member of the group (or the group does not exist), the call returns `CHANNEL_ERROR` with `sub_code: "NOT_GROUP_MEMBER"`.

## Group details and posts

Same endpoint, same `group_url` param:

- `"get_group"`: the group's `name`, `description`, `rules`, `industries`, `member_count`, `created_at`, `is_public`, the account's `membership_status` (`MEMBER`, `MANAGER`, `OWNER`, `NON_MEMBER`), `owners`, `managers` and LinkedIn's `activity_insights` (e.g. `"+354 members in the last week"`). Works on groups the account hasn't joined. 1 credit.
- `"get_group_posts"`: the group feed in LinkedIn's order, members only. Same post fields as [`get_posts`](/api-reference/v2/profiles/get-posts), plus `group_post_urn` and `actor_profile_urn`. `count` up to `100`; pass the returned `cursor` for the next page. 1 credit per 10 results.
