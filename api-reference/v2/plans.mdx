LinkupAPI has two ways to pay. Pick one in [Billing](https://app.linkupapi.com/plans); the API behaves the same, only the limits differ.

| | Per-account plan | Credit plans |
|---|---|---|
| **What you pay for** | Connected accounts | API calls |
| **Usage** | Unlimited on every endpoint except email enrichment | 1 credit per call, 1 per 10 results on lists |
| **Best for** | Products connecting their users' accounts | Low volume, data-only use |

## Per-account plan

One account is one LinkedIn profile, one Gmail, Outlook or IMAP mailbox, or one WhatsApp number. A dedicated IP per account and real-time webhooks are included.

**Price grid (per month)**

| Connected accounts | Price |
|---|---|
| 1 to 10 | $55 flat |
| 11 to 50 | $5.50 per extra account |
| 51 to 200 | $5.00 per extra account |
| 201 to 1,000 | $4.50 per extra account |
| 1,001 to 5,000 | $4.00 per extra account |
| Above 5,000 | $3.50 per extra account |

The price is graduated: 30 accounts cost $55 + 20 × $5.50 = $165. It is capped at **$3,000/month**, the Unlimited plan: from about 640 accounts you pay the flat price and the seat count stops mattering.

**How the month works**

- You are billed upfront each month for your number of seats.
- **Auto-upgrade (default: on).** You can connect more accounts than your seats at any time, nothing blocks. At renewal, the peak number of accounts connected at the same time during the month is billed at the grid price and becomes your new seat count. Seats are never lowered automatically; ask us to reduce them.
- **Auto-upgrade off.** Your seat count is a hard limit: connecting one more account returns `SEAT_LIMIT_REACHED` (402). Use it for a fixed budget.
- Disconnecting an account frees its seat for the rest of the month; the peak already reached still counts at renewal.
- Seats and auto-upgrade are managed in [Billing](https://app.linkupapi.com/plans).

**What still costs credits.** Email enrichment (`/v2/enrich`) is billed in credits on every plan. Without credits it returns `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` (402), everything else keeps working.

## Credit plans

Most actions consume **1 credit**. Actions returning lists (search, connections, inbox) consume **1 credit per 10 results**, rounded up. The exact amount is in `metadata.credits_consumed` on every response, and `GET /v2/credits` returns your balance.

When the balance reaches zero, calls return `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` (402) until the plan renews or you buy a pack. Connected accounts and webhook configurations are kept.

## Switching from credits to per-account

Subscribe to the per-account plan in Billing. Your credit subscriptions stop renewing and the remaining credits stay usable for email enrichment. Connected accounts, API keys and webhooks are untouched: nothing to redo in your integration.

## No active plan

A workspace with neither plan (checkout not completed, or a per-account trial that ended without a card) gets `SEAT_LIMIT_REACHED` (402) on login and on actions, with a message pointing to Billing. See [Error codes](/api-reference/v2/error-codes).
