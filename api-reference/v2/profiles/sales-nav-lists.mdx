## Overview

Three actions on the Sales Nav lists of the account's seat:

- `list_lists_nav`: the seat's lead lists or account lists.
- `create_list_nav`: create an empty list.
- `add_to_list_nav`: add leads to a lead list, or companies to an account list.

To read the content of a list, use [Search People Nav](/api-reference/v2/profiles/search-people-nav) (`lead_list` or a `.../sales/lists/people/<id>` URL) or [Search Companies Nav](/api-reference/v2/profiles/search-companies-nav) (`account_list` or a `.../sales/lists/company/<id>` URL).

<Note>`list_lists_nav` and `create_list_nav` consume **1 credit**, `add_to_list_nav` **1 credit per 10 entities**. Requires a Sales Nav seat on the account. Ignore this if you're on per-seat pricing: usage is unlimited.</Note>

## Request

<ParamField header="x-api-key" type="string" required>
  Your API key for authentication.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account_id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the account to use. Must have a Sales Nav seat.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="action" type="string" required>
  `"list_lists_nav"`, `"create_list_nav"` or `"add_to_list_nav"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="params" type="object">
  <Expandable title="list_lists_nav">
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" default="lead">`lead` or `account`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="ownership" type="string" default="all">`all`, `owned` (lists the seat created) or `editable` (lists the seat can add to).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="offset" type="integer" default={0}>Index of the first list to return.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="count" type="integer" default={25}>Number of lists to return, up to 100.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="create_list_nav">
    <ParamField body="name" type="string" required>List name.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>`lead` or `account`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="description" type="string">List description.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="add_to_list_nav">
    <ParamField body="list_id" type="string" required>The list id returned by `list_lists_nav` or `create_list_nav`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="profiles" type="string[]">For a lead list: up to 25 profile URLs, Sales Nav lead URLs or profile ids.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="companies" type="string[]">For an account list: up to 100 company ids, company page URLs or Sales Nav company URLs.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable title="list_lists_nav">
    <ResponseField name="lists" type="array">
      <Expandable title="list properties">
        <ResponseField name="list_id" type="string">List id.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">List name.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="type" type="string">`lead` or `account`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="entity_count" type="integer">Number of leads or companies in the list.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="description" type="string">List description.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="source" type="string">`MANUAL` for a list the user created, otherwise the kind of list Sales Nav maintains (e.g. `LEADS_TO_FOLLOW_UP`).</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="role" type="string">The seat's role on the list, e.g. `OWNER`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="shared" type="boolean">Whether the list is shared.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="created_at" type="integer">Creation time, Unix ms.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="last_modified_at" type="integer">Last change, Unix ms.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="last_viewed_at" type="integer">Last time the seat opened it, Unix ms.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_results" type="integer">Number of lists returned.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="offset" type="integer">Echoes the requested `offset`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total_available_results" type="integer">Total number of lists of that type.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="create_list_nav">
    <ResponseField name="list_id" type="string">Id of the new list.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string">List name.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string">`lead` or `account`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="description" type="string">List description.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="add_to_list_nav">
    <ResponseField name="list_id" type="string">The list.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="added" type="integer">Entities added.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="already_in_list" type="integer">Entities that were already in the list.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="failed" type="integer">Entities not added.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="results" type="array">One entry per input, in input order: `input`, `profile_id` or `company_id`, `status` (`added`, `already_in_list` or `failed`) and `error` when it failed (e.g. a company sent to a lead list, an unknown list).</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
